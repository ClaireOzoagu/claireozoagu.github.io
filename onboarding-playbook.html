01 — The challengeIt has to pay for itself by renewal

Kora Creative, a fictional 30-person digital marketing agency, has just bought Claude for the whole team.

Account managers lose about three hours per client on monthly reports, and everyone uses AI in their own style, so the Kora voice gets lost.

The budget is tight. In the Managing Director's words: "This has to pay for itself by renewal."

02 — My approachFour phases, one goal

01

Handoff

Before the customer logs in, I learn why they bought, so they never have to repeat themselves.

02

Kickoff

We agree what success means in the customer's words, and who does what by when.

03

Launch

I race them to the first value moment: no blank screens, the whole team brought in, and the dip watched closely.

04

Value Confirmed

I prove the value with their own numbers, so at renewal they say "we can't go back."

05 — Target outcomesWhat success looks like

These are illustrative targets. Kora Creative is a fictional customer and all figures are made up for this concept case study. I don't work for Anthropic and this isn't sponsored.

06 — What this shows about meHow I work with customers

  • Proactive. I reach out first and act on the signals before the customer tells me something is wrong.
  • Outcome-led. Success is defined in the customer's own words, with baselines, owners and dates.
  • Fast time to value. Everything is set up so the first value moment lands in 14 days, not months.
  • AI-adoption fluent. I know how teams actually take up AI tools, and where they give up.
  • Commercially minded. I tie the work back to the budget, the renewal and the next expansion.
