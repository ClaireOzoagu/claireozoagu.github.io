01 About me

Hi, I'm Claire. I keep customers getting value.

I've spent 5+ years in client-facing roles across customer support, client onboarding and operations: owning the relationship after the sale, making sure clients got what they were promised, and fixing the process when they didn't.

At Claire's Unique Cereals, my own business, I built the CRM and ran the proactive follow-up that grew revenue by 70%. That is the heart of Customer Success: know what each customer needs, watch the signals, and step in before a small gap turns into churn.

02 How I help businesses

What I do for your customers.

I help SaaS and service businesses keep the customers they've worked hard to win: onboarding them well, spotting risk early, and building the systems that make good service repeatable.

01

Customer Onboarding & Adoption

I design onboarding that takes new customers from kickoff to first value: clear goals, role-based training, written guides and tracked milestones.

You get: faster time-to-value and fewer early drop-offs.

02

Health Scoring & Churn Prevention

I build health scores from product usage, support tickets and engagement, so your team sees at-risk accounts long before renewal.

You get: problems caught in days, not weeks.

03

CRM Setup & Workflow Automation

I set up HubSpot or Zoho CRM pipelines and automations that turn customer signals into tasks, reminders and follow-ups.

You get: a process that doesn't depend on someone remembering.

04

Customer Journey & Voice of the Customer

I map the customer journey, find the moments that decide loyalty, and set up simple feedback loops that reach management.

You get: customers who feel informed at every step.

05

Training & Enablement

I teach tools in plain language. As a coach I've taught 200+ students, and I bring that to customer training, guides and internal SOPs.

You get: customers and teams who actually use what they paid for.

06

Dashboards, KPIs & Reporting

I build dashboards and KPI trackers that show adoption, account health and risk in one place.

You get: leaders who can see the numbers and act on them.

03 Case studies

Problems I've worked through.

Each case study follows the same frame: the case, the bigger problem behind it, my task, and what I built.

01 Team project · Fintech SaaS

BukkiBooks

Catching silent churn before it costs a renewal.

The case

Okonkwo FinTech Solutions had a customer, Ihuoma Fashion House, that looked active but had quietly stopped using a key feature, inventory reconciliation, for five weeks. The cause was a new store manager who had never been trained.

What I built

A scalable early-warning process: a weighted health score, customer segments by touch level, HubSpot automations that turn warning signs into CSM tasks, a KPI tracker and a risk register.

02 Portfolio project · Retail

Ijeoma Home & Living

A customer journey people can trust, end to end.

The case

A furniture brand with three showrooms whose customers loved the product but felt neglected along the way. Only 39% of orders got a correct written confirmation, and only 44% of customers asked could state their delivery date.

What I built

One tracked journey in Zoho CRM, from first enquiry to after-sales care: written confirmation, production updates, a dispatch docket and a post-install survey that triggers a callback.

  • Zoho Survey
